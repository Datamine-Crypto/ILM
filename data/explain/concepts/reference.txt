Note: Words that point back. "He" points to the man and "she" to the woman of the people just named; "it" points to the thing; "him" or "her" in "went with him" points to the person that the sentence before went with. In a story, "he" points to the person named last who can be a man: a man of the list names, or a person whose name the list does not know; "she" the same for a woman; "they" points to the two people who went somewhere together last. The words point back in sentences of going, taking, dropping, giving and being in a place.
Note: Whether a name is a man's or a woman's comes from the list names; when one of two names is known, the other person is the other one.
