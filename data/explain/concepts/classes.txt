Note: Classes and kinds. A thing of a kind has what every thing of that kind has, and a kind inside a bigger kind passes this on. "Every cat is furry", "each cat is furry", "all cats are furry" and "cats are furry" say the same. A thing of a kind also does what every thing of that kind does: if all mammals give birth to young ones, a bat does give birth to young ones.
Note: When things of one kind are told to have a colour, a thing of that kind that is not told its colour is taken to have the colour told last for its kind.

Reply to "What is A afraid of?" with D: A is afraid of the D.
