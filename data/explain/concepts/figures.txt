Note: Plane figures drawn by a path of commands, as an SVG path draws them (the path is the d of a path element, d = " ... ", in any sentence): move to a point (m), a line to a point (l), and an arc to a point (a, with its two radii first). The points that the path reaches tell the figure: two points and no arc draw a line; three to eight points and no arc draw a polygon of that many corners; the path joins each point to the next one that a line reaches; four corners of which two joined ones are right angles draw a rectangle, and four corners of which two each join two sides of the same length draw a kite (as near as the written digits allow: one part in a hundred); arcs alone with equal radii draw a circle, with two radii an ellipse, and an arc with lines draws a sector.
