Note: Rules told in a story. A told sentence "If someone is big then they are kind." is a rule for any thing: someone, something, it and they name the same thing. "They" and "it" name one thing here, so "they are" says "it is" and "they like" says "it likes". "All big things are kind", "Big things are kind" and "Big, red people are kind" are rules too. A list of qualities ("big and red") says each quality of the same thing.
