Note: A walker on flat ground: where the walker stands, as two numbers (east of the start, north of the start), and the way the walker faces. The walker starts at the start, facing north. A step forward goes the way the walker faces, a step backward the other way, and a step to the left or the right goes to that side without a turn. A turn to the right faces the next way round the compass (north, east, south, west), a turn to the left the way before, and a turn around the opposite way. A step with no way told goes forward. The walker is back at the start when both numbers are nought.
