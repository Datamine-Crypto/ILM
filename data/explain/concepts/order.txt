Note: Comparison and scales. A comparison puts two things in order on the scale of one quality (brave, tall, polite, big).
Note: The order goes on through a third thing; a word with the opposite meaning puts the same things the other way round; the thing at the top of a group is above each other thing of the group. A thing that fits inside another is smaller.
