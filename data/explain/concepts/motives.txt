Note: Needs and what they lead to. A person who needs something goes where it is and takes what meets the need; the need is the reason for the going and the taking.
If A is B and B people go to the C then A will go to the C.
If A is B and B people want C and D is C then A wants the D.
Reply to "Where will A go?" with C: A will go to the C.
Reply to "Why did A go to the C?" with B: A went to the C because A was B.
Reply to "Why did A get the C?" with B: A got the C because A was B.
