Note: Possession and transfer. Taking a thing makes the taker hold it; giving it to another person makes the other hold it.
Note: Dropping, leaving, discarding or putting a thing down ends the holding. A person carries the things whose last holder the person is; two things are two different things, and "the football there" is the football.
Note: In each giving one side gives and the other gets: a teacher teaches and a learner learns, a lender lends and a borrower borrows, a seller sells and a buyer buys, a speaker tells and a listener hears.
