Note: The words of family relations in general words: a son is a male child, an aunt is a female pibling. Source: general knowledge of English.
Note: Pibling (a sibling of a parent) and nibling (a child of a sibling) are the words without a sex, as parent and sibling are.
Son means a male child.
Daughter means a female child.
Father means a male parent.
Mother means a female parent.
Husband means a male spouse.
Wife means a female spouse.
Brother means a male sibling.
Sister means a female sibling.
Grandson means a male grandchild.
Granddaughter means a female grandchild.
Grandfather means a male grandparent.
Grandmother means a female grandparent.
Nephew means a male nibling.
Niece means a female nibling.
Uncle means a male pibling.
Aunt means a female pibling.
