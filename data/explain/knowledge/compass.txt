Note: The directions of a compass: the four main directions and the four between them, each as a step across and a step up; the opposite of each; which way a right turn from each faces. Source: general knowledge.
Builds on opposites.
North is a direction.
South is a direction.
East is a direction.
West is a direction.
North is the opposite of south.
East is the opposite of west.
A right turn from north faces east.
A right turn from east faces south.
A right turn from south faces west.
A right turn from west faces north.
