Note: The wording of the group "lexical" of LoNLI: statements to judge as entailment, contradiction or neutral. A statement about another side of a person (a job, a home, a trait) than the told one is neutral.
Note: Three word pairs that the lessons give are not true in English (irrepressible for irresistible, stranger for unknown, sycophant as the opposite of unmannerly); they are faults of the set and are not taught.
Builds on meaning.
To answer "that animal is a B": entailment if that animal is a B. contradiction if that animal is a C. contradiction if that animal is an C. neutral otherwise.
To answer "that animal is an B": entailment if that animal is an B. contradiction if that animal is a C. contradiction if that animal is an C. neutral otherwise.
To answer "A is B in colour": entailment if A is B in colour. contradiction if A is C in colour. neutral otherwise.
