Note: The wording of the graph form of CLUTRR: a told fact "A daughter B" says that B is the daughter of A, and the question ('A', 'B') asks what B is to A.
Note: Four rules here are conventions of the set and not true in general, so the concept family does not teach them: a spouse of a parent is a parent (in life it can be a stepparent), a spouse of a grandparent is a grandparent, a grandparent of a child is a parent of the parent that the story names, and so is an aunt or uncle a sibling of that parent (in life each can be on the side of the other parent).

If A is a spouse of B and B is a parent of C then A is a parent of C.
If A is a grandparent of B and C is a spouse of A then C is a grandparent of B.
If A is a parent of B and C is a grandparent of B then C is a parent of A.
If A is a parent of B and C is a pibling of B then C is a sibling of A.
