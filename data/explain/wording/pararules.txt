Note: The wording of ParaRules, the rules of ProofWriter put in their own words by people. A sentence about a person (named, or "he" after a name) gives the person each quality that it names: "From a certain angle Dave is blue but he is young" says that Dave is blue and young. A sentence about people in general, with no name and no "he", is a rule: the qualities that it names first are the condition and the quality named last is the outcome ("A kind, round person will surely be red as well." says that if someone is kind and round then they are red).
